Info

UP Karting is compromised of students focused on practical engineering, collaboration, and the development of karting projects.

UP Karting is hosted in the Laboratory of electromechanical energy conversion in the University of Patras. We aim to connect classroom knowledge with practical engineering through real design, manufacturing and testing.

The goal is simple: learn by building, improve through testing, and work together on projects that are real.

Our sponsors help us move forward with components, materials, software, manufacturing services, technical guidance, and support for testing or events.
